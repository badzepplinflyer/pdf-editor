Alex's PDF Editor

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

Alex's PDF Editor is a personal tool for viewing and editing PDF files in a web browser. This page explains what it does with your information. In short, your files stay on your device and in your own Google Drive. The editor has no server of its own and collects nothing about you.

What the editor accesses

  • PDFs you open. Files you choose are read and edited inside your browser. They are not sent anywhere except your own Google Drive, and only when you connect it.
  • Google Drive, if you connect it. The editor asks for the drive.file permission. That lets it create files and folders in your Drive and open only the files it created. It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Your Google account email address. This is read once after you connect so the editor can show which account it's saving to. It stays in your browser.

What is stored, and where

  • In your Google Drive: a "PDF Editor" folder with the PDFs you save, plus a "Working files" subfolder holding copies of the PDFs you opened, the edit files (.pdfedit) that let you reopen a document where you left off, and your saved signature image if you made one.
  • In your browser: a short-lived Google sign-in token, your email address and a copy of your last signature, so you don't have to set these up again each visit. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

What the editor does not do

  • It has no server or database of its own. Nothing is collected, logged, tracked or analyzed by the developer.
  • It does not use cookies for tracking, advertising or analytics.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.
  • It does not use Google user data for advertising, and it does not use it to develop or train AI or machine-learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The editor's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features you see in the editor: saving, listing and reopening your documents.

Third-party services

The page is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its code libraries (PDF.js and pdf-lib) and fonts from public content delivery networks (cdnjs and Google Fonts). Like any website, those providers may receive standard technical information such as your IP address when your browser loads the page. Signing in to Google is handled by Google's own sign-in service.

Removing your data

  • Delete the "PDF Editor" folder from your Google Drive to remove everything the editor saved.
  • Revoke the editor's access any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove the sign-in token and saved signature on that device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
